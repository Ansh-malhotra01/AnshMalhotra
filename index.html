About me

I am Ansh Malhotra, a Computer Science and Technology engineer. I was born on 15 August, the day India celebrates its freedom, and I have wanted to serve my country in uniform for as long as I can remember.

My path began in Jammu and led me to Maharashtra. I faced struggles in my initial days, but every step taught me discipline, patience and the value of hard work. Today I serve as a Company Quartermaster Sergeant in the NCC, volunteer in the NSS and in social events, and work with the Indian Army in Delhi.

My journey

From a hard start to a proud finish, and still climbing.

  1. Born on 15 August

    I was born on the day India celebrates its freedom. From childhood, the uniform and the Tricolour meant more to me than anything else. The wish to serve the nation was with me from day one.

  2. The struggle

    My initial days were not easy. I faced setbacks, doubts and long nights, far from home. Every time, I chose one answer: try again.

  3. Four years of sacrifice

    As a Computer Science and Technology student, I gave up comfort, free time and easy choices. I studied hard, served as Class Representative, and trained in the NCC and NSS at the same time.

  4. The small wins that grew

    I became a Company Quartermaster Sergeant. I led the Kolhapur Group in the Inter Group Competition three times and was selected Best Cadet from the Kolhapur Group for the RDC IGC at Pune. I interned at NITTTR Bhopal and IIITDM Kancheepuram.

  5. Reaching and achieving

    After four years of discipline, I was honoured as the Best Outgoing Student of the CST branch and the Student of the Year of the School of Engineering and Technology (SET), Shivaji University, Kolhapur (SUK). Today I work as an intern with the Indian Army in Delhi, at the Directorate General of Information Systems (DGIS), Ministry of Defence. This is only the beginning.
